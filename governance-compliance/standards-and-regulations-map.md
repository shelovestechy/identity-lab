# Standards and Regulations Map

This page is a practical map for connecting governance and compliance topics to identity and access management work.

It does not try to rewrite standards or legal text.

The point is to translate them into real access decisions: users, groups, approvals, lifecycle changes, external access, sensitive data and audit evidence.

## Map

| Topic | Practical meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [ISO/IEC 27001](./iso-27001.md) | Controlled information security management | Access control, ownership, reviews, evidence |
| [GDPR](./gdpr.md) | Personal data protection | Who can access personal data and why |
| [NIS2](./nis2.md) | Cyber risk and resilience | Incident impact, governance, critical systems |
| [Finnish cybersecurity regulation](./finnish-cybersecurity-regulation.md) | National implementation and practical obligations | Risk management, reporting, management responsibility |
| [Audit evidence](./audit-evidence.md) | Proving access decisions later | Tickets, approvals, reviews, logs |
